Why SilverCare

Bridging Independence & Peace of Mind

Managing multiple medications, physician appointments, and health vitals is overwhelming for seniors and stressful for families. SilverCare connects everyone in real-time.

💊

Smart Medication Timers

Visual, color-coded schedules with morning, afternoon, evening, and bedtime reminders. One-touch confirmation prevents missed or double doses.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family & Caregiver Portal

Family members and visiting nurses can remotely monitor adherence scores, view missed dose alerts, and post shared health notes.

🚨

Instant Emergency SOS

One-touch SOS with a 5-second safety buffer immediately alerts family and caregivers with live GPS coordinates and first-responder medical data.

🩺

Doctor Visit Preparation

Keep track of doctor appointments, generate printable 14-day adherence dossiers, and list essential questions to ask the physician.

🔊

Voice-Guided Accessibility

Built-in Text-to-Speech reads today's medications aloud for seniors with vision challenges. Includes high-contrast mode and font scaling.

📊

Adherence Analytics

Track weekly and monthly compliance trends, streak milestones, and low-stock inventory refill alerts before prescriptions run out.
